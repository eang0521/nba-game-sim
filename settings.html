Season Simulation
Rating Uncertainty
Before each season sim, apply independent N(0, σ) noise to every player's 2PS, 3PS, DEF, and REB ratings (rounded to the nearest integer). Models real-world variance in player performance.
Noise Std Dev (σ)
Points of rating variance per stat per player. σ = 3 means ~95% of changes stay within ±6 rating points.
3
Home Court Advantage
The home team receives +1 to all four ratings (2PS, 3PS, DEF, REB) for the duration of each game.
Injuries
Each game carries a chance of an in-game injury (20% per roll, re-rolled for multiples). Injured players sit out a random number of future games — regular season and playoffs alike — before returning.
